Ch. 01 — The Craft

Built to the rifleman's intent,
not a factory spec sheet.

Every rifle that leaves this shop is assembled to a single customer's needs — their chosen chambering, their barrel contour, their stock, their trigger. No two are the same, and no two should be.

The work begins with a conversation. Hunting the high country at 800 yards? A lightweight mountain build with a carbon fiber barrel. Competing in PRS? A chassis system in one of the most popular hunting and PRS calibers, tuned for consistency. One thing that sets North Shore apart — quick turnaround without cutting corners on quality.

Every rifle is accuracy-tested and delivered — because precision is a claim that has to be proven on paper.
