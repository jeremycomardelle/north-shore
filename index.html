What we build

Three kinds of rifle. One standard.

Every rifle is built to your shooting, your discipline and your budget — then tested before it ever leaves the bench.

Competition rifles

Precision rifles built for PRS and NRL Hunter — set up for fast, repeatable performance from every position and barricade.

Hunting rifles

Accurate, dependable rifles that carry well and shoot when it counts — built around the game you chase and the country you hunt.

.22 precision rifles

Rimfire builds for PRS22 and NRL22 — low cost per round, big fun, and the best way to sharpen your fundamentals.

Built for the match

Rifles that belong on the leaderboard.

How a build works

From first conversation to first group.

  • 01

    Tell us how you shoot.

    Discipline, distances, budget and preferences — we start with what you actually need the rifle to do.

  • 02

    We spec and build it.

    Action, barrel, chassis, trigger and optic choices matched together, then assembled and fitted by hand.

  • 03

    Tested before it ships.

    Every rifle is shot and verified before it goes home with you, ready to run.

About

A gunsmith who shoots what he builds.
