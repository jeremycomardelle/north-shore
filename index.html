Custom rifle builds

A rifle built around how and where you shoot. Hunting, PRS, NRL Hunter or rimfire, we work with you on the caliber, barrel, stock or chassis, trigger and optic, then assemble and fit everything by hand.

  • Lightweight hunting rifles for the field
  • Competition rifles for PRS and NRL Hunter
  • Rimfire builds for PRS22 and NRL22

Barrels & chambering

Accuracy starts at the barrel. We help you choose the right caliber, length, contour and twist rate for your bullets and your goals, then cut and headspace the chamber to tight tolerances.

  • Barrel fitting and rebarreling
  • Custom chambering, cut to spec
  • Twist rate matched to your ammunition

Rifle actions

The action ties every part of the rifle together. We true and blueprint actions so the receiver, bolt and barrel line up as one system, for smoother operation and more consistent results.

  • Action blueprinting and truing
  • Face and tenon threading
  • Bolt work and full action prep

Muzzle & barrel threading

Add a suppressor, muzzle brake or compensator with threads cut concentric to the bore. Good alignment keeps devices seated properly and protects against baffle strikes.

  • Common pitches such as 1/2x28 and 5/8x24
  • Custom pitch and diameter on request
  • Barrel inspected and measured first

Triggers

A clean, consistent trigger makes a big difference in how well you shoot. We install and tune quality aftermarket triggers to the pull weight and feel that suits your discipline.

  • Trigger selection and installation
  • Pull weight and reset adjustment
  • Set up for competition or hunting

Laser engraving

Make it yours. Permanent laser engraving for names, logos, artwork and functional markings such as caliber, serial or load information.

  • Custom text, logos and artwork
  • Caliber and identification markings
  • Crisp and long-lasting

Results

Built to your intent, not a factory spec sheet.

Every rifle that leaves the shop is assembled to a single customer's needs — their chambering, their barrel contour, their stock, their trigger. No two are the same, and no two should be.

The work begins with a conversation. Hunting the high country at 800 yards? A lightweight mountain build with a carbon fiber barrel. Competing in PRS? A chassis system in one of the most popular hunting and PRS calibers, tuned for consistency. What sets North Shore apart is quick turnaround without cutting corners on quality.

Every rifle is accuracy-tested before it's delivered, because precision is a claim that has to be proven on paper.
